# AMLA & EPPO — Connected by Design. Proven by Evidence.

**European institutional cooperation · 2026**  
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Publisher:** Chaintriviere Consulting  
**Category:** Strategic Perspectives & Industry Commentary

The AMLA–EPPO Working Arrangement signed on 17 November 2026 connects financial intelligence with criminal investigation at Union level. This analysis examines the governance framework, information-sharing mechanisms, control-design elements and operational dependencies that remain subject to later instruments.

**Central thesis:** The arrangement connects institutions, but only evidence will demonstrate effective operational cooperation.

## Scope

The analysis covers:

- Institutional mandates and the legal basis for cooperation.
- Information exchange, joint analyses and the hit/no-hit mechanism.
- Data protection, information handling and traceability.
- Technical standardisation and implementation dependencies.
- Control design, oversight, accountability and evidence of operating effectiveness.
- Implications for obliged entities, RegTech providers and assurance functions.

The article distinguishes provisions stated in the Working Arrangement from professional interpretation and illustrative assurance considerations. It does not present the latter as new legal or contractual obligations.
